# Mascot provenance

Asset: `dist/assets/coach-knight.png`.
Name: Lens (working name). A wooden chess knight with reading glasses.
Created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool, not the CLI. Transparent background retained. The image is decorative; all learning content remains readable as text. The earlier owl concept was rejected by the user and is not included in the app or portable source package.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: original mascot for Chess Lens, a modern adult chess learning dashboard. Create ONE anthropomorphic wooden CHESS KNIGHT piece, recognizably a traditional Staunton horse-head chess knight with a sculpted arched neck and circular turned pedestal base. No full horse body, no legs, no wings, no feathers, no bird, no owl. Warm dark walnut carved wood, ivory inlay details, small round brass reading spectacles perched naturally on the horse's muzzle, one understated expressive eye and a relaxed thoughtful half-smile. Sophisticated tactile 3D product illustration, clean modern art direction, friendly and witty without baby-like proportions or giant eyes. Isolated centered full piece with base visible, three-quarter view, generous transparent margins, soft studio light. Espresso walnut cream brass palette, crisp recognizable silhouette at small UI size. No text, no logo, no background, no books or extra props. Genuine transparent background.
